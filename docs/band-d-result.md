## What to do next

`band-d.next.heading`
> Collect one number, then reconsider

`band-d.next.body`
> Don't hire anyone yet. Don't buy any tools. Just collect data for a week.

**Here's exactly what to do:**

1. Pick the single most repetitive task in your firm — the one that makes people groan when it comes up
2. Time each instance: how long does it actually take?
3. Count the instances: how many times does it happen in a week?
4. Note who does it: is it your most expensive person doing low-value work?

**Do the math:**

- Hours per week = (time per instance) × (instances per week)
- Dollar cost per week = (hours per week) × (hourly rate of person doing it)
- Dollar cost per year = (weekly cost) × 50

**That number** — the real, measured cost of the repetitive work — is what makes the case later. Without it, you're guessing. With it, you have a business case.

Once you have that number, retake the assessment. If your score moves into Band C or higher, you'll have a clearer picture of what's actually missing.
