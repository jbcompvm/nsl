## What to do next

`band-c.next.heading`
> Fix the missing piece, then come back

`band-c.next.body`
> The assessment will tell you which specific thing is missing in your case. Fix that first — it's work you can do without us. Then retake the assessment in a few weeks.

**Depending on what's missing:**

- **If you have no system of record:** Pick a CRM or shared system and get your core data into it. This is foundational work that pays dividends whether or not you ever automate anything.

- **If you have no named owner:** Identify the person who will run the workflow after handover. This isn't about job titles — it's about who picks up the phone when something breaks on a Friday afternoon.

- **If you have no decision-maker access:** Map who actually approves spending in your firm. If it's a committee for everything under $10,000, that's a structural constraint that won't change with a better pitch.

- **If you have no clear bottleneck:** Pick the single most repetitive task in the firm and time it for a week — how many times it happens, how long each one takes, who does it. That number is what makes the case later.

## What your specific gap means

`band-c.gap.system-of-record`
> **No system of record:** Your data lives in inboxes, spreadsheets, or people's heads. Before automation, you need a single source of truth. Pick a CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, or similar — all have free tiers) and migrate your core entities: contacts, companies, deals. This isn't automation — it's basic operational hygiene. Do this first.

`band-c.gap.no-owner`
> **No named owner:** You don't have someone who will pick up the phone when the workflow breaks. This isn't about hiring — it's about naming the person who owns it after handover. If nobody owns it, nothing we build will survive month two. Identify that person first.

`band-c.gap.no-approval`
> **No decision-maker access:** You can't approve roughly $3,000 without a committee. This is a structural constraint. Map who actually approves spending in your firm. If it's a committee for everything under $10,000, that won't change with a better pitch. Work within that constraint or change the structure.

`band-c.gap.no-problem`
> **No clear bottleneck:** You have multiple problems with no ranking. Pick the single most repetitive task in the firm and time it for a week — how many times it happens, how long each one takes, who does it. That number is what makes the case later. It costs you nothing to collect.
